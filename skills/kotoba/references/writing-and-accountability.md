# Writing and accountability

## Choose a useful shape

For a decision brief, lead with the decision or recommendation and its material
condition, then the supporting facts. BLUF (bottom line up front) is a name for
this ordering, not a reason to remove uncertainty. A narrative, acknowledgment,
or sensitive reply may need a different opening.

For a handoff, SBAR means situation, background, assessment, recommendation.
Keep the assessment distinct from observed facts and the recommendation distinct
from an agreed action. Use only the parts the task needs; short messages rarely
need four headings. Never fill an empty heading with invented information.

## Drafting from notes

Notes are a source with gaps, not permission to complete the story. Preserve who
reported a fact and whether the writer observed it. Turn fragments into sentences
without supplying an owner, deadline, magnitude, or explanation the notes omit.
Put a material gap where the reader encounters the claim or action it affects.
When two notes conflict, show the conflict or request clarification rather than
selecting the more convenient value. A draft can be useful while incomplete.

Example notes: “Test: 7/80 failed; staging only. Cause unknown. Rerun proposed;
owner and date unset.”

Possible draft: “In the staging test, 7 of 80 checks failed. The cause is unknown.
We propose a rerun; its owner and date remain to be agreed.”

This preserves the denominator, environment, uncertainty, and proposed status.
It does not establish production reliability or a commitment to rerun.

## Concrete does not mean invented

Delete an empty abstraction when it adds no recoverable proposition. Replace it
with a concrete action, mechanism, result, or example only when the source supplies
it. Otherwise ask a question or leave a visible gap such as “[measured result not
supplied].” Polishing “we drove transformational synergy” must not manufacture a
project, outcome, metric, or owner.

A sentence that could describe any company unchanged is a prompt to check for
missing substance, not proof of dishonesty. Meaningful aspirations and specialist
terms can remain. Do not use a buzzword blacklist.

## Meaning-preservation check

Compare each substantive original proposition against the draft, and each new
assertion against its source. Deleting repetition is fine; deleting a limitation
is a meaning change. Check these dimensions rather than matching exact words:

| Dimension | Preserve | Failure example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quantities | Values, units, denominators, baselines, time periods, and direction. | “3 of 40” becomes “3%.” |
| Attribution | Speaker, source, and reported versus observed status. | “The vendor estimates” becomes “We guarantee.” |
| Uncertainty | May, estimates, ranges, missing evidence, and unknown causes. | “May help” becomes “will solve.” |
| Scope | Population, test setting, exclusions, and relevant dates. | A pilot result becomes an all-customer result. |
| Commitments | Who agreed to what, authority, timing, conditions, and explicit noncommitment. | “If approved” disappears. |
| Voice | Requested register, characteristic phrasing, and first-person perspective. | A plain note becomes an impersonal executive proclamation. |
| Firmness | Boundaries, refusals, required actions, and warranted urgency. | “Do not share” becomes “Try not to share.” |

An aspiration (“We aim to reduce waiting”) is not a promise. A request (“Please
review by Tuesday”) is not the recipient's acceptance. A suggestion (“Could Lee
review?”) is not Lee's assignment. A conditional offer remains conditional.
If an edit corrects a demonstrable arithmetic error, explain the correction;
do not claim that checking arithmetic verifies the underlying measurements.

Keep useful specialist language; unusual terms and non-native phrasing are not
proof of empty meaning. Do not invent motives, soften documented harm into a
misunderstanding, or escalate ordinary feedback into an accusation. An explicit
boundary can stay direct: “I cannot take weekend work” need not become “I may be
able to help at weekends.”

## Evidence and limits

These checks are Amaterasu design safeguards, not empirically established effects
of Kotoba. Shorter writing and a model's self-rating do not prove preservation.
Independent readers and proposition-level annotations must test that hypothesis.

- [Army main-point-first guidance](https://tjaglcs.army.mil/Periodicals/The-Army-Lawyer/tal-2019-issue-1/Post/5907/No-2-Find-Your-Yoda)
  (2019, “Rule #9”) is operational guidance. The project foundation inspected
  that passage; this phase does not claim a new source review or causal evidence
  for AI-assisted workplace writing.
- [SBAR systematic review](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/8/8/e022202) (2018)
  concerns healthcare. The foundation recorded direct retrieval failure and
  retained a plan-supplied assessment of heterogeneous, limited-quality evidence.
  No stronger evidence claim or corporate transfer is established here. Kotoba
  uses the structure as an optional organizing aid, not a validated intervention.

Examples above are original synthetic illustrations, not evaluation results.
