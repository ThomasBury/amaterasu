# Commitments and conduct

## Extract the obligation without strengthening it

Keep the original wording beside the summary. For each possible commitment,
record speaker, recipient, promised action, conditions, timing, acceptance
criteria, and actual authority. Use “unspecified” where the material is silent
and “disputed” where accounts conflict. Record the evidence location and date.

“We will consider a promotion if the trial succeeds” commits at most to
consideration under a condition; it does not promise promotion. “I will recommend
you” promises a recommendation, not the decision of the approving panel. A request
is not recipient acceptance. Silence does not establish agreement. A specific
promise may be clear while its fulfillment remains unknown.

Check which conditions were met, which deadline applied, and whether a later
change was accepted. Do not retroactively erase a missed deadline because a
new one was proposed. Do not infer dishonest intent from lateness or failure.

## Describe what the evidence supports

| Material | Bounded observation | Relevant response option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specific, firm correction of a work error | Feedback on the work; firmness alone does not establish misconduct. | Clarify the requested correction or explain a factual disagreement. |
| A single explicit threat tied to reporting a concern | Identify the threat and its condition directly; repetition is unnecessary. | Preserve the message privately and consider a trusted support route; direct confrontation is optional. |
| Multiple dated records of public humiliation | Describe the documented words, occasions, and pattern without diagnosis. | A boundary or support request can address repeated conduct. |
| One recollection or clipped quotation | Attribute the report and identify what context is missing. | Seek context if safe and useful; do not dismiss the experience or turn it into a settled allegation. |
| Conflicting accounts or incomplete policy | Leave the conflict visible; a title cannot settle it. | Request the relevant record or clarify authority and applicable policy. |

Separate evidence that words were written from evidence that an allegation
inside those words is true. Do not demand corroboration before acknowledging a
reported concern, but do not describe the report as independently established.
Keep event dates distinct from the dates on which someone recorded or recalled
them. Chronology can explain a change without proving intent or causation.

## Fit the response to the concern

Offer only useful options. Clarification resolves missing criteria or ownership;
a boundary states what conduct or request the user will accept; private factual
notes preserve chronology; seeking support addresses power, repetition, or
urgency. Do not assume a particular reporting channel is available, confidential,
or safe. Use supplied policy and the user's constraints, leaving gaps visible.

Possible clarification: “Are you committing to a recommendation or to the final
decision, and when would that happen?” Possible firm boundary: “Please address
the work issue without personal insults.” These are optional drafts, not a
requirement to confront someone or a guarantee of a favorable response. Where
there is an immediate threat, prioritize the user's safety and suitable local
support over perfect documentation. Never send, report, or escalate automatically.

## Evidence and limits

These are original design safeguards and synthetic examples, not a validated
conduct classifier or legal guidance. The project's foundation review of
[NAQ-R research (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1080/02678370902815673) recorded that
full-text retrieval failed; no detailed validation claim is made here. The
foundation distinguishes workplace-exposure questionnaires from interpreting
individual messages. This phase adds no new literature review and does not
claim that questionnaire findings establish these instructions' effectiveness.
Workplace-expert review of pilot labels and independent behavioral evaluation
remain necessary. A source citation, author walkthrough, or passing file check
cannot replace either.
